Quick Reference

Six mechanisms at a glance

📄CLAUDE.md
Drop a CLAUDE.md in your project root. Claude reads it every session for persistent instructions and conventions.
🧠Skills
Save reusable instruction sets (skills) and load them on demand across different projects.
⚡Hooks
Attach actions to lifecycle events (file change, pre-command, post-command) for automation.
🔌MCP
Connect Claude to external APIs, databases, and services through the Model Context Protocol.
🧩Subagents
Delegate complex subtasks to independent sessions for parallel or isolated execution.
📦Plugins
Package MCP servers, skills, and hooks into shareable plugins for team or community distribution.
