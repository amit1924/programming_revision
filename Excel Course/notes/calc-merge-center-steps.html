Step-by-Step: Merge & Center a Title

Goal: turn "Sales Report" in A1 into one big centered heading across columns A→D

METHOD 1 of 3
1

Open your sheet & find the heading

In A1 sits your text. The empty cells B1, C1, D1 to the right are where you want the title to spread across.

Select the cells you want to merge

Click A1 and drag right to D1. The whole range A1:D1 lights up green. This tells Calc "merge these 4 cells".

Selected range = A1:D1 (4 cells). Only A1 keeps its text.

3

Open Format on the menu bar

On the top bar, click Format. A dropdown will drop down.

4

Go to Merge Cells

In the dropdown, hover Merge Cells. A sub-menu appears with the merge tricks.

5

Choose Merge and Center Cells

Click Merge and Center Cells. Done! The 4 cells become 1, and "Sales Report" jumps to the center.

B

Method 2 — if "Merge and Center" isn't there

Older Calc versions only offer plain Merge Cells. Reach the same result with 2 extra steps.

1

Select A1:D1

Drag from A1 to D1 as before.

2

Format → Merge → Merge Cells

This joins them but does not center yet.

3

Center it now

Click the Center button or Format → Cells → Alignment → Horizontal: Center → OK.

C

Method 3 — make your own shortcut

Calc has no default key for merge — but you can add one in seconds.

1

Open keyboard settings

Menu Tools → Customize → Keyboard.

2

Bind a key

Find "Merge and Center Cells", then press e.g. Ctrl+Shift+M, click Assign. Now it's one key.

D

Step-by-Step: Alignment

After (or without) merging, tweak how text sits inside its cell.

1

Select the cell(s)

Click the cell, or range of cells, whose alignment you want to change.

2

Open Alignment dialog

Menu Format → Cells, then the Alignment tab. (Fast way: Ctrl+1 → Alignment.)

3

Pick a Horizontal alignment

ABAB
1LeftCenter 3RightJustified

Guide: Left = text · Center = titles/headers · Right = numbers · Justified = stretch to both edges.

4

Pick a Vertical alignment

ABc
1 Top Middle Bottom

Use Middle for titles and headers to look neat.

5

Click OK and check

Your title is now centered and vertically middle. Add Bold and a bigger font (14–18 pt) to finish the table header look.

30-Second Revision Checklist

To merge & center
  • 1) Select cells (A1) → drag → (D1)
  • 2) Format → Merge Cells → Merge and Center
  • 3) or Toolbar button → Merge and Center
To align
  • Format → Cells → Alignment tab
  • Horizontal: Center / Left / Right
  • Vertical: Top / Middle / Bottom
  • OK to apply
Warnings
  • Merging keeps only the upper-left cell's data
  • Calc warns you — click Yes only if OK
  • No default shortcut; make your own (Tools → Customize)
