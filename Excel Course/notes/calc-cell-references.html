Cell References

Every formula points at cells. When you copy it, does the pointer move (relative) or stay stuck (absolute)? See it on real rows, columns and cells below, one step at a time.

Goal: a price list where each row totals its own Qty × Price

Write =B2*C2 in D2. It means "take B2 (5), multiply by C2 (10)". No $ signs → relative.

Watch it MOVE

Because it's relative, everything shifted down one row: D3 auto-becomes =B3*C3 and shows 7×20=140. It "followed" the new row.

Step-by-Step: Absolute references ($)

Goal: multiply EVERY row by the SAME rate locked in cell G2

1

Put the constant rate in G2

Type 0.15 (15%) into G2 — the cell we must NEVER lose when copying.

DEG
1TotalTaxRate
250=D2*$G$20.15
2

Type =D2*$G$2 in the Tax cell (E2)

The two $ lock column G and row 2 → $G$2 can never move.

3

Copy it down to row 3

Watch: D3 changed (relative, moved to D3) but $G$2 stays glued in place. That's the whole point of $.

DEG
1TotalTaxRate
2507.50.15
3140=D3*$G$2
4

The bug if you FORGET the $

Without $, copying makes it =D3*G3 — but G3 is EMPTY, so the tax silently becomes 0. Story of many broken sheets!

A1 no $ — moves when copied. Default.

Mixed

A$1 row · $A1 column.
