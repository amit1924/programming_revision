Today, yesterday, tomorrow & current time

NOW() and TODAY() are the two live anchors

1

TODAY() = today's date, no time

In A2 type the formula, the cell shows only the date.

2

NOW() = date + time

Format that cell as date & time (menu Format > Cells > Number).

3

±1 = a day

Dates count in whole days, so subtract 1 / add 1 gives yesterday and tomorrow.

0.5 of a value = 12 hours. So =NOW()+0.5 is noon/next day — time rides along on the same day-number.

Years / months / days between a start and today

2

Units

"Y" years · "M" months · "D" days · "YM" leftover months · "YD" leftover days

3Full years=DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"Y")…

e.g. "36 years 3 months 8 days" — split across the three cells.

Plain days is just subtraction: =B2-A2 → the number of days between two date cells.

Working days only

NETWORKDAYS counts · WORKDAY finds the date — both skip weekends & holidays

1

COUNT working days inside 01–10 May

=NETWORKDAYS(start, end, holidays)

ABC
1StartEndHolidays in F2:F3
22025-05-012025-05-102025-05-01
3No holidays=NETWORKDAYS(A2,B2)…
4With holidays=NETWORKDAYS(A2,B2,$F$2:$F$3)…

Row 3 ignores the two holidays; row 4 subtracts them. Weekends drop out in both.

Make a row of day numbers

Shade each day cell with one condition

Select the day cells below a task and set conditional formatting. Mark the cell green when the day sits inside the task's span:
