How Each KPI Is Computed

Same idea in Excel — these are SUMIFS / XLOOKUP style.

Total Revenue

Add up Revenue only for the rows that match your filters.

=SUMIFS(Revenue, Region, F2, Product, F3)

Avg Order Value

A derived KPI — two KPIs divided. Revenue ÷ Orders.

=SUM(Revenue) / SUM(Orders)

Best Product

The product with the biggest revenue in the filtered set.

=XLOOKUP(MAX(sums), sums, products)

Top Region

The region with the biggest revenue — "where should we push?"

=SORT(SUMIFS(...), 2, -1) → row 1

Spot the trap: click Region = North + Product = Coffee — only 2 rows count, but the Avg Order Value card still works because it divides matching revenue by matching orders. A formula that forgets the filter would divide the whole table and give a wrong KPI.
