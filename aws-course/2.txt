🎯 The one thing I want you to remember

Whenever you build any website—whether it's www.amit.com, Facebook, Amazon, or Flipkart—the architecture always starts with the same idea:

🏡 VPC = Your company's private land.
🏘️ Public Subnet = Where web servers that users visit live.
🔒 Private Subnet = Where databases and internal services live.
🌍 Internet = Customers access only the public part.
🗄️ Database = Hidden behind the web server, never exposed directly.

This "company campus" mental model is the foundation for understanding AWS networking and matches the way the chapter explains VPCs, subnets, gateways, and routing.

🧠 One sentence to remember

When someone visits www.amit.com, they never connect to "AWS" directly. Their request reaches your EC2 server, which is just a rented computer inside your VPC. That computer runs your web server (like Nginx or Apache), serves your website, and securely communicates with your database in the private subnet.
